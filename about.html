About Us

The first shoulder-specialized center in Egypt & the Middle East.

Founded by Dr. Rana Shehata, Canadian Physio Center brings together Canadian physiotherapy protocols, Asian alternative therapy, and bone osteopathy under one diagnosis-first standard of care.

Our story

Diagnosis first. Always.

Canadian Physio Center was built around one idea: no treatment should begin without a real diagnosis. Every patient — whether they've come for a chronic shoulder injury or a first-time assessment — gets a full physical evaluation before any protocol is designed. That protocol draws on Canadian physiotherapy standards, Asian alternative therapy practices from Malaysia, Vietnam, and Indonesia, and manual bone osteopathy, combined under one roof across our two branches in New Cairo and Sheikh Zayed.
