Who we are

Canadian Physio Center is the first shoulder-specialized physiotherapy center in Egypt & the Middle East, founded by Dr. Rana Shehata.

We assess before we treat. Every patient gets a full physical assessment first, then a customized protocol built on Canadian physio standards, Asian alternative therapy, and bone osteopathy — not guesswork.

What we do

Eight specialized treatment tracks, each with its own diagnosis-to-recovery protocol.

From assessment through to full recovery, every service is built around the same diagnosis-first philosophy — internationally certified, evidence-based, and tailored to the patient in front of us.

01

Assessment

A full physical assessment to reach an accurate diagnosis before any treatment protocol is designed.

 02

Osteopathy (Bone Alignment)

Manual bone alignment techniques to correct structural imbalances and relieve chronic pain at the source.

 03

Shoulder Treatments

The first shoulder-specialized treatment protocols in Egypt, built on Canadian and international rehab standards.

 04

Sports & Athletic Services

Injury recovery and performance rehab protocols built for competitive and recreational athletes.

 05

Spine Injuries

Specialized assessment and rehab protocols for spinal injuries, disc issues, and chronic back pain.

 06

Canadian Physio Protocols

Evidence-based physiotherapy protocols certified by the Canadian Academy of Pain Management.

 07

Asian Alternative Therapy

Traditional alternative therapy practices from Malaysia, Vietnam, and Indonesia, integrated with modern rehab.

 08

Posture Correction

Targeted correction programs for postural imbalances that cause chronic neck, shoulder, and back pain.

Our commitment

We hold every protocol to internationally certified standards — diagnosis first, always.

We recognize the impact our clinical decisions have on every patient who walks through our doors.

1

Our clinical standards

Canadian Physio Center operates under protocols reviewed against the standards of the Canadian Physiotherapy Association, EUSSER (European Union for Shoulder & Elbow Rehabilitation), Physio Academy New Zealand, and the General Physical Therapy Syndicate of Egypt.

Before any treatment begins, a thorough assessment is conducted for every patient. Every protocol is evidence-based and reviewed against the latest research from these accrediting bodies.

2

Delivering accessible, patient-first care

Our strategy connects a diagnosis-first philosophy with real accessibility — direct WhatsApp booking, same-week appointments, and two branches so patients can choose whichever is closer.

Our impact starts with every patient who walks through our doors. We focus on:

  • Full assessment before treatment
  • One-on-one treatment time
  • Direct WhatsApp booking
  • Same-week appointments
  • Two convenient branches
  • Real recovery, not just symptom relief
