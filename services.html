Services

Eight specialized treatment tracks.

Each service has its own diagnosis-to-recovery protocol — internationally certified, evidence-based, and tailored to the patient in front of us.

01

Assessment

A full physical assessment to reach an accurate diagnosis before any treatment protocol is designed.

 02

Osteopathy (Bone Alignment)

Manual bone alignment techniques to correct structural imbalances and relieve chronic pain at the source.

 03

Shoulder Treatments

The first shoulder-specialized treatment protocols in Egypt, built on Canadian and international rehab standards.

 04

Sports & Athletic Services

Injury recovery and performance rehab protocols built for competitive and recreational athletes.

 05

Spine Injuries

Specialized assessment and rehab protocols for spinal injuries, disc issues, and chronic back pain.

 06

Canadian Physio Protocols

Evidence-based physiotherapy protocols certified by the Canadian Academy of Pain Management.

 07

Asian Alternative Therapy

Traditional alternative therapy practices from Malaysia, Vietnam, and Indonesia, integrated with modern rehab.

 08

Posture Correction

Targeted correction programs for postural imbalances that cause chronic neck, shoulder, and back pain.
